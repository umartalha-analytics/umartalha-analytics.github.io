## Billing Delinquency Analysis — Glow Insurance
**Organisation:** Glow Insurance (Professional Work)
**Tools:** Tableau
**Type:** Individual — Operations Analytics

### Problem
Carrier billing schedules across the agency lacked a centralised tracking system,
making it difficult to identify delinquent accounts, monitor payment patterns, and
prioritise follow-up actions across multiple carriers.

### Approach
- Consolidated billing data across carriers into a single analytical view
- Built an interactive Tableau dashboard to track billing delinquency status
  by carrier, policy, and time period
- Designed for operational use by the agency team to support daily
  billing management and carrier reconciliation workflows

### Key Features
- Delinquency breakdown by carrier and billing cycle
- Interactive filters for time period, carrier, and account status
- Visual flagging of high-priority delinquent accounts for follow-up

### Business Impact
Gave the operations team a single source of truth for billing delinquency,
replacing manual tracking and enabling faster identification of at-risk accounts.
